# Vantage ARF Article 8 funds: what the SFDR documents say

Source: the product page https://www.allianzgloballife.ie/products/Vantagearf.html. It has 1 product-level disclosure (28/04/2026) and, for each of the 10 funds, a pre-contractual disclosure and a periodic report (reference period to 09.2025). The PDFs and extracted text are in `docs/regs-research/sfdr-pdfs/`.

The product disclosure says 77% of the product's fund range is Art. 8 and 23% is Art. 6. The Art. 8 funds are the 10 below.

## What every fund shares

- **Exclusions on direct investments.** These apply across the funds, with some thresholds varying by fund.
  - Severe breaches of the UN Global Compact, OECD Guidelines and UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights.
  - Controversial weapons.
  - More than 10% of revenue from weapons or military equipment.
  - More than 10% of revenue from thermal coal extraction.
  - Utilities with more than 20% of revenue from coal.
  - Tobacco production, or more than 5% of revenue from tobacco distribution.
  - Sovereigns with an insufficient Freedom House score.
- **Best-in-class scoring.** Issuers get a score from 0 to 4.
  - Strategy Select: at least 90% of the portfolio is scored.
  - Dynamic Multi Asset SRI, AFI and Best Styles: at least 70% (SRI) or 90% (AFI, Best Styles) of the portfolio has an SRI rating.
  - SRI funds also reduce the universe by at least 20%.
- **No fund invests in fossil gas or nuclear activities that comply with the Taxonomy.**
- **All consider PAIs.** The PAI indicators are covered through exclusions and the internal score, and through engagement and voting.
  - **Greenhouse gas:** emissions, carbon footprint, GHG intensity, fossil fuel exposure.
  - **Biodiversity, water, waste:** data coverage is low, so these are handled through the UN Global Compact exclusion.
  - **Social and employee matters:** UN Global Compact breaches, board gender diversity, controversial weapons.
  - **Sovereigns:** social violations, via Freedom House.
- **Alignment with E/S characteristics.** At least 70% of assets (SRI funds) or 90% (Strategy Select) are used to attain the characteristics.

## Mapping to Part E (S1–S4)

| Question | Can the funds support it? |
|---|---|
| S2 A: EU Taxonomy | Weakly. Every fund commits to only 0.01%, and actual alignment is about 1–4.5%. |
| S2 B: sustainable investments | Yes. There are real committed minimums from 2% to 30%, and actuals of 25–62%. |
| S2 C: PAI | Yes. All funds consider PAIs, but the "which PAIs" detail is thin (see S4). |
| S3: minimum share | A: no fund can meet the lowest option (10%). B: only Best Styles commits to 30% or more. On actuals (not guaranteed), 6 of the 9 funds with data reach 30%, and the Strategy Select funds do not. |
| S4: which negative effects | The four groups map to what the funds consider. Greenhouse gas is the strongest. Biodiversity, water and waste are only covered indirectly. |
