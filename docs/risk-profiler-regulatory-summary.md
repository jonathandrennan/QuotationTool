# Risk Profiler: why it is compliant

Summary for senior management, 23/10/2026. Checked against the legal texts. Compliance is still to sign off.

## In short

- **What it is:** A short questionnaire inside the Quotation Tool. It gives a risk rating from 1 to 6, which brokers use to choose funds in our products.
- **Who uses it:** Brokers only. All our business is advised broker business, and we do not sell direct.
- **Who gives the advice:** The broker. They use the rating alongside their own fact find, can lower it with a recorded reason, and write the Statement of Suitability.
- **Why that is allowed:** The law puts the suitability duty on whoever gives the advice, which is the broker. Our duties as insurer are about product governance and giving brokers clear, accurate information. The tool meets those.
- **Market practice:** Zurich, Aviva, Irish Life and Standard Life all give brokers a risk profiler. Ours does the same job and is at least as thorough.

## Who is responsible for what

| | Allianz (insurer) | Broker (adviser) |
|---|---|---|
| Gives the advice | No | Yes |
| Full fact find (income, assets, debts, health, dependants) | No | Yes |
| Assesses suitability and signs the Statement of Suitability | No | Yes |
| Decides the final risk rating | No (the tool suggests one) | Yes |
| Provides a clear, accurate, tested tool | Yes | No |
| Sets the target market for each product and fund | Yes | No |
| Keeps records of the advice | Supports this (the tool's record) | Yes |

The law confirms this split. The insurer's duty to monitor brokers "shall not extend to the general regulatory requirements with which insurance distributors have to comply when carrying out insurance distribution activities for individual customers" (Delegated Regulation 2017/2358, Article 8(4)).

## Regulations that do not apply, and why

| Regulation | Why it does not apply |
|---|---|
| Consumer Protection Code Regulations 16–19 for the Investment Bond | Regulation 11(3) switches them off for advice on insurance-based investment products. The IDD rules above apply instead |
| MiFID II and the ESMA suitability guidelines | They are for investment firms. They do not bind insurers or insurance brokers. We use ESMA only as a good-practice benchmark |
| PRIIPs Regulation for the PRSA and ARF | Pension products for retirement income are outside PRIIPs, so they have no KID. The Investment Bond does have KIDs. PRIIPs governs the KID, not the risk profiler |
| Appropriateness test for sales without advice (IDD Article 30(2)) | All our business is advised, so the full suitability test applies instead |
| Direct-to-consumer digital rules (Consumer Protection Code Regulations 38–41) on Allianz | We do not engage clients directly. **This changes if the profiler is put on our public website for clients to use alone** |

## What is missing

### Sustainability: the broker assesses it, but we must give them the data

- **The broker's job:** For the Investment Bond, the broker must ask whether the client has sustainability preferences and, if so, how much: a minimum share in EU Taxonomy-aligned investments, a minimum share in sustainable investments, or which harmful effects the funds should take account of (Delegated Regulation 2017/2359, Articles 2(4) and 9(4), as amended by 2021/1257). For the PRSA and ARF the Code only says to gather "any sustainability preferences" (Regulation 16(3)(e)). This can sit in the broker's fact find, so the profiler does not need to ask it.
- **Our job (gap):** As manufacturer we must give brokers the information to match funds to those preferences (Delegated Regulation 2017/2358, Articles 5 and 8(3), as amended by 2021/1257). In practice that means, for each fund: the Taxonomy-aligned %, the sustainable investment %, and whether it considers principal adverse impacts. The tool shows only each fund's SFDR article (6 or 8).
- **Wording risk (gap):** When the broker records "Yes", the Statement of Suitability goes on to say which funds are SFDR Article 8. An Article 8 fund does not automatically meet a client's preferences. A product must not be recommended as meeting preferences it does not meet (2359 Article 9(6)). The wording should not imply a match, or should state the three figures above.
- **The tool's Yes / No:** Keep it, but describe it as a record of the broker's finding, not as the assessment. The fuller questions (Part E) are built and can be switched on if brokers want them.

### Other gaps

| Item | Whose job | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Demands and needs test (IDD Article 20) | Broker | Nothing in the tool. Say in broker guidance that the profiler does not cover it |
| Full financial information: income, assets, debts | Broker | The two capacity questions are not a substitute. Say so in broker guidance |
| Client confirming their answers are accurate (Code Regulation 16(13)) | Broker | Confirm the broker's fact find declaration covers the profiler, or add a confirmation the client ticks at the end (not pre-ticked) |
| Importance of capital security (Code Regulation 16(7), PRSA and ARF) | Broker | Covered in part by the loss and objective questions. Broker guidance should say the fact find covers it |
| Vulnerable clients (Code Regulations 34–37) | Broker | The tool flags age 65 or over with a high rating, and low knowledge. The broker decides |
| Joint applicants | Broker | The tool profiles one person. Brokers need guidance for joint policies |
| Keeping the record | Broker | The tool does not store profiles centrally. The broker keeps the pack as their record (2359 Article 19) |
| Version control and sign-off of the scoring | Allianz | Name an owner, and keep each version and its test results |
| Broker guidance note | Allianz | One page covering what the tool does and does not do. This closes most of the rows above |

## Before launch

1. **Compliance:** sign off this summary, and confirm that the ARF is a pension product and so outside the IDD investment-product rules.
2. **Client testing:** test the questions with a small group of clients and keep the results (Code Regulations 38 and 65).
3. **Wording:** change "your adviser will review your risk profile at least once a year" to "should be reviewed", as we cannot promise what the broker will do.
4. **Data Protection:** confirm the GDPR roles between us and the broker.
5. **Public website:** do not publish the profiler for direct client use without a separate review.
6. **Fund ratings:** check that the tool's 1 to 6 bands line up with the fund risk ratings in the KIDs.
7. **Sustainability data:** add each fund's Taxonomy %, sustainable investment % and principal adverse impacts to the fund information. Fix the Statement of Suitability wording so it does not imply that Article 8 means the preferences are met.
8. **Broker guidance note:** write the one-page note described above.
