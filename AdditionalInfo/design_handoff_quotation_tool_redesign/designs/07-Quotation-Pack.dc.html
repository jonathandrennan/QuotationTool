Your attitude to risk

Based on the risk profiling we carried out with you, your attitude to investment risk corresponds to a risk rating of 4 (Medium) on the 1 to 7 Summary Risk Indicator scale. You have told us that a fall of up to 20% in a single year would not change your plans, as you do not expect to draw on this fund for at least 18 years.

Your sustainability preferences

You told us you have sustainability preferences. All of the recommended funds are classified under Article 8 of the Sustainable Finance Disclosure Regulation.

Our recommendation

We recommend the Allianz Vantage Non-Standard PRSA (A5), with a transfer of €20,000, a single contribution of €50,000 and regular contributions of €500 a month, invested in your own selection of four funds. The indicative risk of this portfolio is 4, in line with your risk rating.

Why we believe this is suitable for you

  • Consolidating your previous employer's pension and your savings into one PRSA keeps your retirement planning in one place, with contributions you can vary.
  • Why the strategy or funds suit the client's attitude to risk and term to retirement

Charges and remuneration

The total effect of the charges on your plan is equivalent to reducing the assumed growth rate by 1.95% per annum. We will receive initial remuneration of €2,950 and trail remuneration of 0.25% a year of your fund value. Full details are set out in the Charges section and the Remuneration Statement in this pack.
